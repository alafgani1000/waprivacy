Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from our users.

Do I need to create an account?

No, absolutely not. WA Privacy Shield operates 100% locally. There is no signup, no login, and no premium subscriptions. Just install it and use it instantly.

Does this work on mobile phones?

No, this extension is specifically designed for Desktop Chromium browsers (Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Brave) running WhatsApp Web on a laptop or PC.

Can anyone steal my chat data?

No. Our extension has zero tracking scripts, no external server connections, and zero third-party dependencies. Your data physically cannot leave your computer.
